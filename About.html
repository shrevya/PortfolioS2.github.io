About Me

Hi there!👋
Welcome to my portfolio!

I'm a curious mind who loves playing detective with people data. A Neurosciences degree brought me into HR from an unexpected angle, and it shapes how I work: understand what makes people tick, then find the answer in the data. Today I channel that into people analytics by building dashboards, digging into trends, and turning messy data into insights teams can act on. Currently pursuing my CIPD, I'm growing toward an HR career that blends sharp analytics with genuine, people-first impact. Off the clock, you'll find me racing the clock in escape rooms, getting a little too invested in true crime documentaries, working out a piece on the piano, building strength through calisthenics, and losing hours to messy, colorful oil-pastel abstracts.

Shrevya Panicker, IN 📍

I work at the intersection of people and data, across people ops and analytics — turning scattered, messy HR data into dashboards, insights, and better-run processes that teams and leaders can act on. What drives me is the "why is this happening?" question: getting under a problem, reading the patterns, and turning something tangled into something clear and decision-ready. And the payoff isn't analysis for its own sake — it's helping real people and real teams make better calls..😀
